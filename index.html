GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your Mintys motorcycle.

Do you deliver anywhere on Koh Samui?

Yes. We offer free island-wide delivery to your hotel, villa or accommodation on Koh Samui.

Are helmets provided?

Yes. Helmets are provided with your rental.

What are the rental prices?

Honda Click: ฿350 daily, ฿2,100 weekly or ฿6,000 monthly. Honda PCX: ฿450 daily, ฿2,500 weekly or ฿9,000 monthly.

How do I book a bike?

Choose your bike, then message us on WhatsApp or email bookings@mintysmotorcycles.com with your dates, bike choice and accommodation details.

Are you open 24/7?

Yes. Mintys Motorcycles is open 24/7, so you can contact us whenever you need us.

Can I extend my rental?

Yes, subject to availability. Contact us before your original rental ends and we'll arrange an extension where possible.

What happens if I have a problem with the bike?

Contact us straight away on WhatsApp and we'll help arrange the next step.
